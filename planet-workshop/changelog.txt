Version: 0.0.5
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Test play: land on the designer's preview planet as a character and play it, with its own gravity,
      pressure, solar power and day and night, awake enemies and a landing kit. Options: starter kit, research,
      free hand-crafting, enemies. "Back to the designer" (or dying) ends it.
    - Checks: a new designer section listing what could make a planet hard or impossible to play (rocket silos
      or landing pads that can't be built, buildings that can't be built, no resources, an own ore that never
      generates, pentapods without swamp, no water, recipes that make nothing or need fluids in the wrong
      machine, missing things, research that needs itself, an unused pack...), each with a "Go there" button.
      The bottom of the window sums them up, and Get code mentions any warnings.
    - The preview runs the starting-resources safety net, as the real planet does.
    - A planet that is already loaded shows its real ores in the preview and test play.
  Bugfixes:
    - Importing a hand-written code that left parts out could break the designer window.

Version: 0.0.4
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Designer production sections: the planet's own ores and fluid wells (with their look and colour), its
      science pack (ingredients, machine, time, made only here, spoiling), its recipes, and its research (the
      discovery's needs and cost, and its own technologies with prerequisites, cost, infinite levels and rewards).
    - Ingredients, results and costs can be things from the game or the planet's own ore, fluid or pack.
    - Planets can have their own fluid resources (a tinted well or vent giving the planet's own fluid).
    - Science packs can spoil, like Gleba's.
    - The preview shows a planet's own ores and wells in their base look.
  Changes:
    - The designer's sections are a sidebar in three groups (World, Planet, Production).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.0.3
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - The Planet Designer scenario (New game > Scenarios): design planets in a window with tabs for the name,
      terrain, plants and rocks, resources, enemies, physics, space route and looks, beside a live preview you can
      zoom, reseed and fly over. Several planets per save; New, Copy and Delete.
    - Get code makes the planet's code (selected, ready to copy, and saved to script-output/planet-workshop);
      Import code loads planet codes to look at or change.
  Changes:
    - Every planet now generates from one shared terrain kit whose numbers live in the planet's map settings, so
      the designer's preview uses exactly the rules the finished planet gets.
    - Ground tiles mix closer to their shares (3:1 now gives about 74%, was 84-89%).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.0.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Terrain: land shapes (lakes, big lakes, island, archipelago, continents), a liquid (water, lava, oil,
      ammonia, slime or none) with an amount, and any mix of natural ground tiles from any planet, each with a share.
    - Cliffs, plants and rocks in vanilla styles with an amount; decorations from the ground's home planets.
    - Enemies: biters, pentapods (they nest in the swamps) or demolishers, with an amount and matching pollution.
    - Physics: freezing (buildings need heat) and lightning storms.
    - Look: the planet's picture is chosen automatically (the planet most of the ground comes from, otherwise the
      closest colour), and the sky (lighting and ambient sound) separately.
    - Every starting resource gets a patch near the landing spot (each planet has its own patch set), with a
      safety net that adds a small patch if a seed buries one.

Version: 0.0.1

  Features:
    - Proof of concept: planet codes pasted into the "Planet codes" startup setting become real Space Age planets,
