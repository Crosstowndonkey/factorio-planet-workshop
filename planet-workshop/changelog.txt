Version: 0.9.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - How it works: a guide to the whole process (design, check, get the code, save, add it in the main menu,
      play) opens the first time the designer starts, and from the new ? button.
    - The Get code window lists the steps to play a planet, including saving the designer game first and that
      codes are added from the main menu.
    - Physics: "Same as" buttons copy a vanilla planet's gravity, pressure, magnetic field, solar power, day
      length, freezing and lightning.
    - Resources: "Add the resources of" buttons add the resources a vanilla planet has.
    - "Making the preview..." shows under the preview while it's being made.
    - The sidebar's sections have tooltips saying what's in them.
  Changes:
    - The window fits smaller screens and bigger UI scales (the preview gets smaller).
    - Research prerequisites show their names next to their icons; a pack that never spoils says "never";
      clearer wording for the liquid amount, research units and adding a resource.

Version: 0.9.1
Date: 2026-11-12
  Bugfixes:
    - The game couldn't start when a planet's research listed the same science pack twice, or the same
      prerequisite twice; repeats are now merged.
    - The game couldn't start when research needed research that needed it back; the link closing the loop is
      left out (and reported).
    - The game couldn't start when a recipe made by hand (Made in: crafting) used a fluid; such recipes are now
      made in machines with pipes, and the designer warns about it.
    - The game couldn't start when a description was longer than 200 characters.
    - A hand-made start area with a value the game refuses (from a hand-edited code) could stop the game when
      the planet was first visited; such things are now skipped.
    - New games, respawns and test play could put you in a lake in the middle of a hand-made start area; they
      now find dry land.
    - The designer's Terrain and Resources sections broke for ground tiles or resources from mods that aren't
      installed; they now show them as "not in this game".
  Features:
    - Checks: research paid in something no lab takes.
    - A starting planet set after a game began now says it only counts for new games.
    - The Planet Designer scenario has a picture in the scenario list.

Version: 0.0.8
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Get code opens a Share window: tick other planets to make a planet pack (one code, several planets).
    - Save share picture: a picture of the planet's preview with a card about it (its look, name, author,
      description, ground, resources, enemies, gravity, day length, its own pack and ores), saved to
      script-output/planet-workshop to post with its code.
    - Remixes: a planet imported from someone else's code can be changed; when it's shared it becomes your
      remix, with its own id (it won't replace the original), you as the author, and "Based on ... by ...".
      Copy of someone else's planet does the same.
    - Credits: a planet's description in game says who made it and who it's based on; /planet-workshop lists it.
    - Mod checks: codes list the other mods a planet uses. Loading or importing one without them says which
      mod is missing (things from it are left out), and the designer's Checks warn about it.

Version: 0.0.7
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Start on your own planet: a new "Starting planet" startup setting (type a loaded planet's name). New
      Freeplay games then start there: the crash site, the first landing, the intro message, the charted start
      area and respawns are on that planet, its discovery is already researched, and Nauvis is a planet to fly to
      later. Works with hand-made start areas.
    - Designer: "Made to be a starting planet" (General) adds checks for what a new game needs (iron, copper,
      stone, fuel, oil, water, air for burner buildings, no freezing or lightning), and test play gets a
      "New game" starter kit.
  Info:
    - The starting planet is only used in new Freeplay games, and not together with Any Planet Start.

Version: 0.0.6
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Hand-made start areas: a new Start area section. Edit start area opens Factorio's map editor on the
      planet, with a circle around the landing spot (32 to 96 tiles); paint ground and water, place or clear
      ores, trees, rocks, plants, fish, cliffs and enemies, then Save or Cancel. An ore brush places the planet's
      own ores. Only the changes are kept in the code, so it stays small.
    - A planet with a hand-made start area is the same world in every game (the one in the preview), with the
      changes on top, so they always fit the land around them.
    - A check warns about start area things that don't exist in this game.

Version: 0.0.5
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Test play: land on the designer's preview planet as a character and play it, with its own gravity,
      pressure, solar power and day and night, awake enemies and a landing kit. Options: starter kit, research,
      free hand-crafting, enemies. "Back to the designer" (or dying) ends it.
    - Checks: a new designer section listing what could make a planet hard or impossible to play (rocket silos
      or landing pads that can't be built, buildings that can't be built, no resources, an own ore that never
      generates, pentapods without swamp, no water, recipes that make nothing or need fluids in the wrong
      machine, missing things, research that needs itself, an unused pack...), each with a "Go there" button.
      The bottom of the window sums them up, and Get code mentions any warnings.
    - The preview runs the starting-resources safety net, as the real planet does.
    - A planet that is already loaded shows its real ores in the preview and test play.
  Changes:
    - The Planet Designer scenario comes with its own small map, so starting it no longer shows the map generator.
  Bugfixes:
    - Importing a hand-written code that left parts out could break the designer window.

Version: 0.0.4
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Designer production sections: the planet's own ores and fluid wells (with their look and colour), its
      science pack (ingredients, machine, time, made only here, spoiling), its recipes, and its research (the
      discovery's needs and cost, and its own technologies with prerequisites, cost, infinite levels and rewards).
    - Ingredients, results and costs can be things from the game or the planet's own ore, fluid or pack.

    - Science packs can spoil, like Gleba's.
    - The preview shows a planet's own ores and wells in their base look.
  Changes:
    - The designer's sections are a sidebar in three groups (World, Planet, Production).

Version: 0.0.3
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - The Planet Designer scenario (New game > Scenarios): design planets in a window with tabs for the name,
      terrain, plants and rocks, resources, enemies, physics, space route and looks, beside a live preview you can

Version: 0.0.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Terrain: land shapes (lakes, big lakes, island, archipelago, continents), a liquid (water, lava, oil,
      ammonia, slime or none) with an amount, and any mix of natural ground tiles from any planet, each with a share.
    - Cliffs, plants and rocks in vanilla styles with an amount; decorations from the ground's home planets.
    - Enemies: biters, pentapods (they nest in the swamps) or demolishers, with an amount and matching pollution.
    - Physics: freezing (buildings need heat) and lightning storms.
    - Look: the planet's picture is chosen automatically (the planet most of the ground comes from, otherwise the
      closest colour), and the sky (lighting and ambient sound) separately.
    - Every starting resource gets a patch near the landing spot (each planet has its own patch set), with a
      safety net that adds a small patch if a seed buries one.
