Version: 0.0.3
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - The Planet Designer scenario (New game > Scenarios): design planets in a window with tabs for the name,
      terrain, plants and rocks, resources, enemies, physics, space route and looks, beside a live preview you can
      zoom, reseed and fly over. Several planets per save; New, Copy and Delete.
    - Get code makes the planet's code (selected, ready to copy, and saved to script-output/planet-workshop);
      Import code loads planet codes to look at or change.
  Changes:
    - Every planet now generates from one shared terrain kit whose numbers live in the planet's map settings, so
      the designer's preview uses exactly the rules the finished planet gets.
    - Ground tiles mix closer to their shares (3:1 now gives about 74%, was 84-89%).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.0.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Terrain: land shapes (lakes, big lakes, island, archipelago, continents), a liquid (water, lava, oil,
      ammonia, slime or none) with an amount, and any mix of natural ground tiles from any planet, each with a share.
    - Cliffs, plants and rocks in vanilla styles with an amount; decorations from the ground's home planets.
    - Enemies: biters, pentapods (they nest in the swamps) or demolishers, with an amount and matching pollution.
    - Physics: freezing (buildings need heat) and lightning storms.
    - Look: the planet's picture is chosen automatically (the planet most of the ground comes from, otherwise the
      closest colour), and the sky (lighting and ambient sound) separately.
    - Every starting resource gets a patch near the landing spot (each planet has its own patch set), with a
      safety net that adds a small patch if a seed buries one.

Version: 0.0.1
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Proof of concept: planet codes pasted into the "Planet codes" startup setting become real Space Age planets,
      each with its own space route, discovery technology, ores, science pack, recipes and technologies.
    - Broken or partly broken codes never stop the game from loading; problems are listed in chat and with
      /planet-workshop.
